interesting $\mathbb Z_2$ portals.
It does not claim Hysterical causation for any observed asymmetry.

\section{Master theorem}
If a supercritical odd Hysterical mode $Y$ couples through an odd neutral portal
to an activity kernel $K\ge0$, and washout-weighted transport $\Phi$ is odd and
strictly positive on nontrivial nonnegative sources, then on opposite histories
\[
\eta[Y_+]=-\eta[Y_-]\neq0
\]
whenever overlap ($K>0$ and $\dot Y\neq0$ concurrently) holds.
The $W\widetilde W$ and $R\widetilde R$ channels are named instances of this theorem.

\section{Catalogue}
A five-column ledger lists symmetries with odd coordinate, neutral portal,
early-universe activity, and whether Nature shows a broken yield.
Highlights: strong CP and dark $\mathbb Z_2$ have portals without broken yields;
baryon/CP do show broken yields; weak parity is already chiral in the SM
Lagrangian.

\section{Weak-parity speculation}
Optionally, a left--right / mirror-symmetric parent Lagrangian could be reduced
to the observed chiral SM by Hysterical selection of one hand.
That is an interpretive gloss, not a theorem.

\section{Conclusion}
